There is a particular hour before the day becomes useful. The coffee is still too hot, the street is mostly empty, and every surface seems to have a little more room around it.

This is a place for keeping those fragments: photographs, short observations, and the things that would otherwise disappear between one day and the next.

![An abstract placeholder for a morning photograph](/images/placeholder.svg)

The shape of the site will change as the collection grows. For now, this is enough: one note, one image, and a reason to come back.
