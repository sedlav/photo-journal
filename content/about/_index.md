this is a journal about building a good, grounded life in South Minneapolis. i’m a husband and dad of two, a data engineer by trade, and happiest outside with my family—riding bikes, camping, watching birds, and exploring trails.

i care about the natural world and the communities and cities we build around it. that means thinking about how we get around, what we consume, how we spend our time, and how to raise kids who know and love where they live.

this is a place to document that life: family, bikes, nature, the outdoors, technology, and the effort to live a little more deliberately.

the name reflects my journey. i was born and raised in Chile, in the austral south, and came to make Minnesota my home. del austral al boreal means “from the south to the north.”
